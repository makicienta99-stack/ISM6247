1. Connect your FAU Canvas calendar feed

  1. Log in to Canvas at canvas.fau.edu and open Calendar in the left menu.
  2. At the bottom of the right sidebar, click Calendar Feed.
  3. Copy the link (it looks like https://fau.instructure.com/feeds/calendars/user_….ics) and paste it below.

🔒 The feed link is stored only in this browser. It is sent to this site's Netlify function only to fetch the calendar (Canvas blocks direct browser requests).

2. …or upload an .ics file

Open the feed link in your browser to download it, then upload the file here. Works even without the Netlify function.
